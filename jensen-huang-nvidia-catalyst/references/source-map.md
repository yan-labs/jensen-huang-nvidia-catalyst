# Source map - Jensen Huang / NVIDIA public dynamics

## Source hierarchy

**S-tier: primary sources**
- NVIDIA Newsroom: `https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/`
- Jensen Huang official bio/headshot: `https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/bios/jensen-huang`
- NVIDIA Blog: `https://blogs.nvidia.com/`
- NVIDIA Technical Blog: `https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/`
- NVIDIA Investor Relations: `https://investor.nvidia.com/`
- NVIDIA Events / GTC / On-Demand:
  - `https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/events/`
  - `https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/`
  - `https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/on-demand/`
- NVIDIA YouTube: `https://www.youtube.com/@NVIDIA`
- SEC EDGAR for NVIDIA: `https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1045810`

**A-tier: major media / wire services**
- Reuters, AP, CNBC, Bloomberg, Wall Street Journal, Financial Times, Nikkei,
  The Information, Fortune, TIME.
- Use these for interview quotes, policy remarks, supply-chain confirmations,
  and customer/capex context. For paywalled sources, capture only public snippets
  unless accessible through normal browser access.

**B-tier: specialist trade press / data vendors**
- SemiAnalysis, Tom's Hardware, AnandTech, ServeTheHome, EE Times, TrendForce,
  DigiTimes, Wccftech, The Register, TechCrunch, VentureBeat.
- Useful for technical context and early product/channel clues. Verify important
  claims through NVIDIA, customers, suppliers, or major media before scoring.

**C-tier: social clips / newsletters / forums**
- X, LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube clips, conference attendee posts, newsletters.
- Treat as lead generation unless the clip has a visible source, timestamp, and
  enough context to verify the quote.

Customers: MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, ORCL, TSLA, xAI, OpenAI-related partners,
CoreWeave, Dell, Supermicro, HPE, Lenovo.

Suppliers / ecosystem: TSM, ASML, ARM, AVGO, MRVL, MU, Samsung, SK Hynix,
SMCI, DELL, HPE, ANET, ETN, VRT, CEG, VST.

Use these only as second-order confirmation. A customer capex statement can
confirm demand; a supplier capacity comment can confirm bottlenecks.

## Source caveats

- Jensen often uses broad strategic language. Do not over-score visionary
  comments unless paired with product timing, customer names, supply capacity, or
  policy detail.
- Media summaries can compress technical terms. Preserve the original quote or
  official transcript where possible.
- Conference clips can be miscaptioned or old. Verify date and event.
- Do not scrape around paywalls or private events. Use public sources.
